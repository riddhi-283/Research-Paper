In the realm of lung cancer prediction and diagnosis, various studies have explored the application of machine learning techniques to enhance early detection accuracy. "A Multifaceted Approach to Lung Cancer Diagnosis Using Machine Learning Techniques" by Ongolu Bindu Sri Sai et al. (2024) delves into the utilization of SVM, decision tree, KNN algorithms, and principal component analysis on a comprehensive dataset to identify significant factors influencing lung cancer prognosis. The findings underscore the potential of machine learning algorithms in improving the identification and prediction of lung cancer, emphasizing the critical role of early detection for better patient outcomes and treatment interventions.

On a different front, "Lung Cancer Detection and Classification using CT Scan Image Processing" by Nusraat Nawreen et al. (2021) presents a novel approach centered on CT scan image processing for accurate lung cancer detection and classification. Through preprocessing techniques, edge detection, feature extraction, and SVM classification, the study demonstrates significant advancements in detecting lung tumors and estimating severity levels. This work highlights the synergy between image processing and machine learning in bolstering the accuracy of lung cancer prediction, particularly through the utilization of Artificial Neural Networks.

Further contributing to the landscape of lung cancer prediction is the research conducted by Dr. Satyanarayana Murthy Nimmagadda et al. (2024) in "Lung Cancer Prediction and Classification Using Machine Learning Algorithms." This study focuses on developing an automated system for lung cancer prediction and classification by leveraging machine learning algorithms and image processing techniques. Notably, the Random Forest algorithm showcased remarkable accuracy of 99.7% in classifying different types of lung tissue, underscoring the potential of advanced ML approaches in enhancing lung cancer diagnosis accuracy and ultimately improving patient outcomes.
